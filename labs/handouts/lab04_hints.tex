\textbf{Step 5 --- make your own clothes.}\; {\small The VAE's latent is 2-D, so a ``latent point''
is just \textbf{two numbers}. Every point decodes to something clothes-like --- pick a point, see
what the VAE draws, then change the numbers and re-run. Roughly $-3\ldots 3$ stays near the clothes
the model knows; go further out for stranger results.}

\textbf{Blank 1 --- \texttt{MY\_LATENT} (a latent point).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] \opt{MY\_LATENT = [1.5, -0.8]}\; --- any \textbf{list of two finite numbers}. This is
    the format the cell wants; try \opt{[0, 0]}, \opt{[-2, 1.3]}, \opt{[3, 3]}, \ldots
  \item[(b)] \opt{MY\_LATENT = 1.5}\; --- a single number, not a list; trips the check
    (\opt{len(MY\_LATENT) == 2}).
  \item[(c)] \opt{MY\_LATENT = [1, 2, 3, 4]}\; --- four numbers; the latent is only 2-D, so this also
    fails the length check.
\end{itemize}
{\small (a) is the format --- \emph{any} two finite numbers work, so explore. (b) and (c) are the
wrong shape and fail the \opt{len == 2} \texttt{assert}.}

\vspace{3pt}\hrule\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{Step 6 --- draw anything.}\; {\small Edit the 16$\times$16 ASCII grid: \opt{'\#'} is ink,
\opt{'.'} is blank. The rule is only about \textbf{format} --- keep \textbf{every row exactly 16
characters} and use only \opt{'\#'} and \opt{'.'}. Any shape is fair game; the clothes-VAE will redraw
whatever you give it as the nearest garment.}

\textbf{Blank 2 --- \texttt{DOODLE} (the drawing).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[(a)] Any shape you like --- the shipped \textbf{spiral}, your initials, a smiley, a star ---
    as long as every row is exactly 16 chars of only \opt{'\#'}/\opt{'.'}. This passes and gets
    reimagined as clothing.
  \item[(b)] Rows of \emph{unequal} length, or a stray character like \opt{'x'} or a space --- trips
    the grid sanity-check \opt{assert} (every row must be 16 chars of only \opt{'\#'}/\opt{'.'}).
  \item[(c)] An empty grid (all \opt{'.'}) --- runs fine, but there is nothing to reimagine; draw
    \emph{something} for a fun rebuild.
\end{itemize}
{\small (a) is the goal --- there is no wrong drawing, only the format. (b) errors out at the parse
check. (c) is legal but boring.}
